# 0002: Collaborative Sighting Confirmation ("Saw It Too")

We decided that field sightings logged by any attendee during an Expedition populate a live Shared Trip Checklist, but do not automatically increment the personal Life Lists of all attendees. Instead, other attendees can tap a 1-click "Saw it too!" button to verify their own observation. This preserves the personal integrity and pride birders have in their verified Life Lists while keeping collaborative sighting effortless.
