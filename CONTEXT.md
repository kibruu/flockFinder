# FlockFinder Domain Glossary (CONTEXT.md)

## Core Entities

### Birder
A registered member of the platform. A Birder can act as an Expedition Host, a Carpool Driver, a Passenger, or an independent field observer. Every Birder maintains a **Life List** (a cumulative checklist of distinct species identified).

### Hotspot
A designated geographical birding location (e.g., wetland, national wildlife refuge, coastal reserve) characterized by GPS coordinates, habitat types, available amenities, and seasonal target bird species.

### Expedition (Outing)
A scheduled birding trip to a designated Hotspot. An Expedition has a Host, a target date and time, meeting instructions, a list of target bird species, and an associated roster of attendees.

### Carpool Offer
A ride offering published by a Birder (the Driver) for a specific Expedition. Specifies the departure neighborhood / pickup zone, departure time, total vehicle capacity, and currently available seats.

### Seat Reservation
A confirmed booking where a Birder claims an available seat in a Carpool Offer. Decrements available seats; can be cancelled to free up the seat for another Birder.

### Sighting
An observation record logged during an Expedition or casual visit. Contains the bird species, individual count, location coordinates, timestamp, notes, and optional photo.

### Shared Trip Checklist
The collaborative, live-aggregated checklist of all species logged by any attendee during a specific Expedition. Allows fellow expedition members to confirm their own observation with a single tap.
